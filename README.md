### 📌 Featured Engineering Projects

#### 🏢 1. High-Rise Special RC Shear Wall Design & Torsional Optimization
* **Project Type:** Senior Engineering Project | KMUTT
* **Overview:** 3D structural modeling and dynamic response analysis of a 32-story (140.3 m) reinforced concrete high-rise building under Bangkok soft soil seismic criteria (DPT 1301/1302-61) and dynamic wind loads (DPT 1311-50).
* **Key Technical Contributions:**
  * Modeled and evaluated 4 structural shear wall configurations in **ETABS 22** using **Response Spectrum Analysis (RSA)** and **Modal Response Spectrum Analysis (MRSA)**.
  * Mitigated critical torsional eccentricity ($CM$ vs. $CR$) and verified member capacities ($Pu, Mu, Vu, T$) for structural columns and wall piers.
  * Optimized structural efficiency by balancing dynamic lateral stability, material cost, and net usable floor area.

#### 🏗️ 2. Climate-Resilient Mixed-Use Building: Full BIM & Structural Design
* **Project Type:** Academic Engineering Project | KMUTT
* **Overview:** End-to-end BIM and structural engineering design for a low-carbon, climate-resilient mixed-use facility.
* **Key Technical Contributions:**
  * Developed integrated architectural and structural BIM models using **Tekla Structures** and **Trimble Connect**.
  * Designed structural steel framing according to **AISC 360-10** and reinforced concrete flat slab/mat foundation on piles per **ACI 318**.
  * Performed member sizing, drift verification under **DPT 1311-50** wind criteria, embodied carbon assessment, and material cost optimization.
